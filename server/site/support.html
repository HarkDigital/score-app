Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email support@phade.app.

Alerts don't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Phade Scores in your phone's Settings. Alerts are per team: open the team's page, tap Follow, then the bell, and pick which alerts you want.

A game on the Lock Screen

Open a game that hasn't finished and turn on Show on Lock Screen. A game more than 15 minutes away appears 15 minutes before it starts (iPhone on iOS 17.2 or later). To have every game of a team there, turn on Lock Screen under the team's bell.

Widgets (iPhone)

Follow a team first. Then touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, add a widget and choose Phade Scores (iOS 17 or later).

Alerts arrive before the stream shows it

Under a followed team's bell, Delay game alerts holds starts, scores and finals for the time you choose, for all your teams.

Signing in

You don't need an account to use Phade Scores. Signing in (from the menu) only keeps your followed teams, their alerts and the alert delay the same on all your devices. Signing out keeps them on the device you're using.

Where the scores come from

Scores, standings, rankings, stats and news come from ESPN. Phade Scores isn't affiliated with ESPN or any league or team.
