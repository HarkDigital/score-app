Phade Scores

Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Phade Scores (the website and the iPhone and Android apps) has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share your personal information.

On your device

The teams you follow, the league you last looked at, your filters and your alert settings are kept on your device (in the browser's storage, or in the app). They stay there, except for what the apps send to turn on alerts and Lock Screen cards, below.

Scores, stats and news

Your device loads scores, stats, news, team logos and player photos straight from ESPN, and the font from Google Fonts. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services see your device's IP address and the basic details of each request, and their own privacy policies apply to them. We don't receive any of it.

Alerts, Lock Screen cards and widgets (the apps)

When you turn on alerts for a team or put a game on your Lock Screen, the app sends our server (scores.phade.app):

  • your device's push token, a random code Apple or Google gives the app so that notifications reach it on your device;
  • the teams you follow with alerts on, which alerts you chose, and your alert delay;
  • for a Lock Screen card, the game and the card's own push token.

We use this only to send those alerts and keep the cards up to date, through Apple Push Notification service or Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. It isn't connected to your name, your email address or any account.

Widgets ask our server for your teams' games by team only. Nothing about you is kept.

How long we keep it

  • A Lock Screen card is deleted within 12 hours of its game starting.
  • The record of which alerts went out (so none is sent twice) is deleted after two days.
  • Your alert settings are kept while any alerts are on. Turning them all off deletes them, and so does deleting the app (Apple or Google tell us its token no longer works).
  • The server's web proxy keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) to run and secure the server.

Children

Phade Scores isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
